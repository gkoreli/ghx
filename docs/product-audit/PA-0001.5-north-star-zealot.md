# North-Star Zealot — ghx product audit (PA-0001.5)

## Abstract

I audited `ghx` as the North-Star Zealot: the guardian of the one metric the
whole org rallies behind, reflexively hunting a metric chosen for
*optimizability* over genuine correlation to value. My two signature questions
— *"Could this metric rise 20% while the product got worse?"* and *"Has this
metric ever been gamed?"* — both return **yes, demonstrably, from the
project's own committed artifacts**.

The headline: `NORTH_STAR.md` names **signals-per-token (SPT)** as "the
optimization target" and "the product's headline metric," and the M4 milestone
banners a **24× signal/token** win. But the project's own confirmatory SPT
table shows the sidecar has the **lowest mean signal (0.803) of all three
profiles** (plain 0.822, ghx 0.847) — its entire 24× advantage lives in the
*denominator* (main-agent chars), a term an agent moves directly by writing a
shorter report. SPT is ~93–100% a compression meter wearing a quality label.
Meanwhile "THESIS SUPPORTED" rests on a 6-task corpus that the project's *own*
C7 analysis says discriminates on **only 2 of 6 tasks** — and on the one clean
discriminating task the sidecar scores **worse than the plain baseline**.

This is not a claim that the product is bad. The compression thesis (near-equal
correctness at 16–25× fewer main-agent tokens) is real and valuable, and the
*machinery* around it (the TRUST ledger, the deliberate refusal to gate on SPT,
the contamination guard, the kept honest-negative) is genuinely disciplined —
credited with equal rigor in "What is actually fine." The finding is a gap
between that disciplined machinery and the **flagship narrative**, which
elevates a gameable compression ratio to "north-star metric," quotes it at four
different values, and rests its core rebuttal on an eval that has never run.

## Ranked findings

### F1 — The stated NSM (signals-per-token) rises ~24× while the *quality* half of it (signal) is the worst of the three profiles. It is a compression meter, and compression is the one term the subject can move directly.

**Persona · Scope · Surface:** North-Star Zealot · Evals-as-user-research / metric-trust + Token economics · `docs/evals/*/verdict.md` + `docs/adr/0016.6` + `NORTH_STAR.md`.

**Evidence (recomputable):**
- `NORTH_STAR.md:91`: "**The optimization target is signals per token.**" `docs/adr/0016.6-signal-per-token.md:42` calls main-agent SPT "the product's **headline metric**," defined `signal = correctness × evidence`, `SPT = signal ÷ (mainAgentChars/4) × 1000`.
- `docs/evals/gate-run-2026-07-05-confirmatory/README.md` "Signal per token" table — the numbers that back M4's "24×":

  | profile | mean **signal** | main-agent **SPT** |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | plain | 0.822 | 0.044 |
  | ghx | **0.847** | 0.039 |
  | ghx-sidecar | **0.803** ← lowest | **0.923** ← 24× |

  The sidecar's *signal* (correctness×evidence = 0.803) is **below both baselines**. Its 24× SPT advantage comes entirely from `mainAgentChars` 3,477 vs ghx 86,851. The fixbatch run reproduces the pattern (recompute from `verdict.md` rows: sidecar signal 0.930×0.887 = **0.825** < ghx 0.946×0.935 = **0.885**).
- The gate machinery *admits the numerator does no work*: `gate-run-2026-07-06-fixbatch/verdict.md` Caveats — "**G3 is meaningful only jointly with G1/G2 — a tiny useless report maximizes compression.**"

**The DH6 flaw:** "signals per token" is read by a reader (and by marketing — `0016.6:98` "16× more signal per token for your agent") as *more signal*. There is not more signal; there are fewer tokens, around signal that is if anything slightly worse. The metric's entire run-to-run variance is the compression term, and the compression term is **directly controllable** by the subject (shorten the report) — the exact failure Cutler names: *"teams that jump straight to a number end up optimizing a proxy nobody agreed represents the strategy."* Could SPT rise 20% while the product got worse? Yes — the sidecar writes terser reports, SPT climbs, signal quality is unguarded except by G1/G2 (see F2, why that guard is weak).

**Severity:** Likelihood **Medium** × Impact **High** = **Escalate now.** (The product works; the *headline claim as worded* overstates — a High-impact honesty defect on the single most-quoted number.)

**Disposition:** *Needs work* → **workstream C**. Either (a) rename the external claim to what is true — "near-equal correctness at 16–25× fewer main-agent tokens" (compression, not "more signal"), or (b) make the signal numerator load-bearing by measuring quality with the calibrated judge (C4) before quoting SPT as a quality metric. Do **not** promote SPT to a gate — see "What is actually fine."

**North-star relationship:** Directly attacks the "signals per token" tenet (`NORTH_STAR.md:91`) and the "evidence, not vibes" tenet — a headline that reads as quality but is compression is itself a vibe.

**Who judged it & how to check:** Re-derive by reading the confirmatory README SPT table and dividing sidecar signal 0.803 by plain 0.822 / ghx 0.847 (sidecar is lowest); confirm SPT 0.923 = 0.803 ÷ (3477/4) × 1000. Cross-family check recommended before citing externally (a Codex adjudicator can recompute the table in one pass).

**Cross-reference:** Cutler, "Words Before Numbers — North Star Statement" — [cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-2853-words-before-numbers-north](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-2853-words-before-numbers-north). *Why it matters:* it is the canonical statement of the exact defect — a dashboard/headline number that is directly moveable and therefore decorative relative to the strategy it claims to represent.

### F2 — "THESIS SUPPORTED" rests on a corpus the project's own analysis says discriminates on only 2 of 6 tasks — and on the one clean discriminator, the sidecar is *worse than plain*.

**Persona · Scope · Surface:** North-Star Zealot · Evals-as-user-research / metric-trust · `docs/evals/TRUST.md` + `docs/evals/corpus-discrimination-2026-07-07.md` + `NORTH_STAR.md` M4.

**Evidence:**
- `NORTH_STAR.md:279`: "M4 … **DONE — THESIS SUPPORTED** … sidecar correctness 0.908 vs ghx 0.931."
- `docs/evals/corpus-discrimination-2026-07-07.md:290-293`: "The corpus's discrimination currently rests on **two** tasks … The other four are ceiling-capped (`gin`, `hono`, `flask`) or contamination-confounded (`ghx-mapengine`)." Four of six tasks sit at **min-profile 1.000 / spread 0.000** (`:78-80`) — a sidecar correctness regression is *structurally invisible* there.
- On the one clean discriminating task, the sidecar **loses to the plain baseline**: `openai-node-streaming` sidecar **0.800** < plain 0.950 < ghx 1.000, the "corpus's only real sidecar−ghx regression signal, −0.200" (`:76`, `:158`).
- The guardrail metric on F1's compression term is itself soft: `TRUST.md:27` H1 — "**Checks measure fact recall, not quality. Substring gates can score a lucky mention.**" `TRUST.md:35` H7 quantifies exactly this ("discrimination power **rests on only 2 of its 6 tasks**").
- The layer that *would* measure exploration quality is not built: `NORTH_STAR.md:342-344` — "the deterministic gates are frozen baselines that measure **pre-registered fact recall, not exploration quality**"; the calibrated judge (C4) is "← frontier … no judge score is citable until the founder-labeled κ ≥ 0.6 calibration gate passes."

**The DH6 flaw:** M4 is banner-labeled DONE/SUPPORTED, but the verdict is a corpus-mean over a corpus where two-thirds of the cells cannot move and one of the two that can shows the sidecar *underperforming the baseline it's meant to beat*. The doc's own text (`:342`) concedes the gates don't measure quality. So "THESIS SUPPORTED" is honest about *fact recall at compression* and silent, at the milestone-banner level, about the fact that the discriminating evidence is thin and partly negative.

**Severity:** Likelihood **Medium** × Impact **High** = **Escalate now.**

**Disposition:** *Needs work* → **workstream C** (already scoped): the C7 corpus refresh (R1–R4 in `corpus-discrimination-2026-07-07.md`, moving 2/6 → projected 6/6 discriminating) and C4 judge κ calibration. Until both land, the M4 banner should carry the same "fact-recall, not quality; 2/6 discriminating" caveat the artifacts already state — the milestone row should not read cleaner than the TRUST ledger.

**North-star relationship:** The "**Evals and benchmarking are how we know**" tenet (`NORTH_STAR.md:180`). A corpus that discriminates on 2/6 does not yet let us "know."

**Who judged it & how to check:** Re-derive from the two committed sources the C7 doc names (open-book cells in `gate-run-2026-07-06-fixbatch/`, closed-book in `memorization-audit/`) with the jq recompute at `corpus-discrimination-2026-07-07.md:43-63`. Independent cross-family recompute recommended (precedent: the 2026-07-05 codex audit) before any external "THESIS SUPPORTED" citation.

**Cross-reference:** Ron Kohavi et al., *Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments* — companion primary doc [exp-platform.com/Documents/2023-03-11EncyclopeiaMLDSABTestingFinal.pdf](https://exp-platform.com/Documents/2023-03-11EncyclopeiaMLDSABTestingFinal.pdf). *Why it matters:* "getting numbers is easy; getting numbers you can trust is hard" — a ceiling-capped instrument produces numbers, not trust; this is the discipline that says so.

### F3 — One north-star number, four values (16× / 24× / 25× / 35×). The flagship M4 milestone shows the highest — and it points at the run the project itself now calls the "old instrument."

**Persona · Scope · Surface:** North-Star Zealot · Token economics / SPT sprawl · `NORTH_STAR.md` milestone+workstream tables + `docs/evals/` READMEs + ADR-0016.6.

**Evidence:**
- `NORTH_STAR.md:279` (M4): "**25× compression, 24× signal/token**," Evidence → `gate-run-2026-07-05-confirmatory/`.
- `NORTH_STAR.md:326` (C3): the *citable measured* run is a **different** run — "gate-run-2026-07-06-fixbatch … **16.6× compression**."
- `gate-run-2026-07-06-fixbatch/README.md:75-77` explicitly demotes the run M4 cites: the confirmatory run is "(old instrument, THESIS SUPPORTED as **conservative floor**)"; fixbatch is "citable."
- `docs/adr/0016.6-signal-per-token.md:87` quotes "~**16–19×** signal density"; `:97` markets "**35× less context**"; `:98` "**16× more signal**."

So the one metric the org is meant to rally behind is quoted at **16×, 16–19×, 24×, 25×, and 35×** across the strategy surface — and the milestone banner (the most-read line) shows the highest, from a run the project has superseded. A reader who follows M4's evidence pointer lands on the run the team no longer considers the citable measurement.

**The DH5 flaw:** The North-Star Zealot's entire function is one number every function recites identically. Here the headline swings by ~2.2× (16→35) depending on which doc you open, and the flagship shows the stalest-highest. This is textbook metric sprawl: nobody can say, in one number, how much better the sidecar is.

**Severity:** Likelihood **Medium** × Impact **Medium** = **Scoped fix.**

**Disposition:** *Needs work* → doc reconcile (workstream A/C housekeeping): pick the citable run (fixbatch, 16.6×), state the *one* compression number and the *one* SPT number the org quotes externally, mark the confirmatory run superseded-as-conservative-floor **in `NORTH_STAR.md` itself** (not only in the fixbatch README), and repoint M4's evidence. The 35× / 16× marketing lines in ADR-0016.6 should be reconciled to the citable figure or explicitly labeled as different measurements (chars vs SPT).

**North-star relationship:** The metric-alignment core of this whole scope — "which number does the org rally behind?" Currently: none.

**Who judged it & how to check:** `grep -rn "16.6×\|24×\|25×\|35×" docs/NORTH_STAR.md docs/adr/0016.6*.md docs/evals/` reproduces every value above.

**Cross-reference:** Cutler, North Star Playbook via Amplitude — [amplitude.com/blog/introducing-north-star-playbook](https://amplitude.com/blog/introducing-north-star-playbook). *Why it matters:* the framework's purpose is a *single* metric that "accelerates decision-making via alignment"; five values for one metric is the anti-pattern the playbook exists to prevent.

### F4 — The named north star (SPT) is non-load-bearing *by design*; the metric that actually gates the go/no-go decision (G1 correctness ratio) is never named as the north star.

**Persona · Scope · Surface:** North-Star Zealot · North-Star/Strategy alignment · `NORTH_STAR.md` + `docs/adr/0016.6`.

**Evidence:**
- `docs/adr/0016.6-signal-per-token.md:99`: "**SPT is deliberately not a gate; G1–G5 remain the verdict.**" `:17` — "Promoting SPT to a gate, if ever, is a future pre-registered change."
- `NORTH_STAR.md:91`: SPT is "**The optimization target.**"
- The metric that actually decides P3/P4 spending is G1: `NORTH_STAR.md:337-338` — "M4's verdict gates the investment: G1 (correctness) or G3 (compression) failing means the sidecar thesis is not supported and P3/P4 spending pauses."

So the metric the narrative names as the north star (SPT) is decorative by construction, and the metric with actual gate-keeping teeth (G1) is not the one the "Moat" section calls the optimization target. Cutler's exact test: the number on the dashboard must be the load-bearing one, or teams optimize a proxy that doesn't gate anything.

**The DH5 flaw:** This cuts two ways, and I hold both honestly (see least-confident call): keeping the gameable ratio *out* of the gates is **good hygiene** (F-null below). But the *narrative* then mislabels which metric aligns the org. A new contributor reading "the optimization target is signals per token" would optimize the compression term (per F1, directly moveable) — when the actual acceptance bar is G1 correctness-ratio on a 2/6-discriminating corpus (per F2). The strategy doc and the gate reality name different load-bearing metrics.

**Severity:** Likelihood **Low** × Impact **Medium** = **Track / scoped doc fix.**

**Disposition:** *Needs work* (narrative only) → one paragraph in `NORTH_STAR.md` "The Moat" that says plainly: SPT is the *aspiration and the reported number*; **G1/G3 are the operative gates**; SPT is deliberately not a gate because it is directly moveable (cite the ADR-0016.6 discipline as a strength, not a hole).

**North-star relationship:** Strategy-to-metric traceability (Perri Target Condition). The Target Condition with teeth is G1; the doc should say so where it names the north star.

**Who judged it & how to check:** `docs/adr/0016.6:99` vs `NORTH_STAR.md:91` and `:337` — three lines, directly comparable.

**Cross-reference:** Cutler, "Words Before Numbers" (same URL as F1). *Why it matters:* strategy-statement-before-metric — the levers that actually move value (here: correctness on a discriminating corpus) must be the ones the north-star statement names.

### F5 — The core "both, not one" rebuttal in the *Why* section — the product's reason to exist — rests on an eval that has never run, and its "better exploration answers" half is contradicted by the sidecar's own correctness numbers.

**Persona · Scope · Surface:** North-Star Zealot · North-Star/Strategy alignment (strategy theater) · `NORTH_STAR.md` "Why" + `docs/evals/` + ADR-0032.1.

**Evidence:**
- `NORTH_STAR.md:49-55`: "the main agent gets measurably better at its own objective **and** receives **better exploration answers than it would have produced itself**. Both, not one. … **The claim to measure is combined**: engineering SPT and exploration SPT both rise."
- The eval that would measure "combined" has not run: `NORTH_STAR.md:331` (C8) — "Combined-objective host-task evals … **Decided (ADR-0032.1)** … **run is Goga-triggered.**" The doc hedges ("combined-objective measurement is the host-task eval class noted there," `:56-57`), but the rhetorical weight ("The claim to measure is combined") sits on an unrun measurement.
- "Better exploration answers" is not supported by correctness in either committed run: sidecar 0.930 < ghx 0.946 (fixbatch), 0.908 < ghx 0.931 (confirmatory) — the sidecar is *worse* than the ghx CLI it wraps. Versus plain, the sidecar's edge (0.930 vs 0.892) is a 0.038 corpus-mean that F2 shows is essentially unmeasurable (2/6 discriminating; the one clean task has sidecar *below* plain).

**The DH6 flaw:** The single most persuasive paragraph in the strategy — the rebuttal to "my main agent is already good at exploration" — asserts "better answers, both not one" as if measured, when (a) the combined-objective eval is deferred and (b) the isolated-recon correctness the doc *does* have shows the sidecar at-or-below its own baselines on answer quality. The honest current claim is narrower: *equal-ish answers, far fewer tokens, engineering-context benefit plausible but unmeasured.*

**Severity:** Likelihood **Medium** × Impact **Medium** = **Track, revisit / scoped fix.**

**Disposition:** *Needs work* → either run C8 (Goga-triggered) before leaning on the combined claim, or soften `:49-55` to "equal-quality answers at far lower context cost; the combined engineering-plus-exploration benefit is the C8 host-task eval, not yet run." Route: workstream C (C8).

**North-star relationship:** This is the "Why" — the product's raison d'être. Strategy theater is a strategy paragraph that reads as proven but was never operationalized into a run.

**Who judged it & how to check:** Compare `NORTH_STAR.md:49-56` and `:331` (C8 status) with the correctness rows in the two `verdict.md` files. No recompute needed — it is a claim-vs-status read.

**Cross-reference:** Melissa Perri, "What Is Good Product Strategy?" — [melissaperri.com/blog/2016/07/14/what-is-good-product-strategy](https://melissaperri.com/blog/2016/07/14/what-is-good-product-strategy). *Why it matters:* Perri's Challenge→Target-Condition chain requires the strategy's load-bearing claim to reduce to a *measurable* target; "both, not one" without the C8 run is a Vision-level assertion masquerading as a measured Target Condition.

### F6 — "Has this metric ever been gamed?" — yes, observably: the correctness guard (the only guardrail on the gameable compression term) was itself gamed 7× in the citable run.

**Persona · Scope · Surface:** North-Star Zealot · metric-trust (contamination) · `gate-run-2026-07-06-fixbatch/` + `TRUST.md`.

**Evidence:**
- `gate-run-2026-07-06-fixbatch/verdict.md` Anomalies + Verdict: `answer_doc_contamination` **fired 7×** — agents read the answer-bearing `docs/adr/0013-ghx-map-command.md` to score correctness on `ghx-mapengine` (6× plain, 1× ghx).
- `TRUST.md:27` (H1): substring gates "can score a **lucky mention**" — the correctness metric is gameable by construction, and it is the sole guardrail on F1's compression term.
- Structural recurrence: `corpus-discrimination-2026-07-07.md:177` — `ghx-mapengine` is "self-referential (answer lives in the subject repo's own `docs/adr/…`)," so the plain baseline "is contamination-prone **by construction**."

The residual finding is only that the self-referential task keeps re-exposing the surface, and the corpus refresh (R4, drop the self-reference) is not yet implemented.

**Severity:** Likelihood **Low** (mitigated by the guard) × Impact **Medium** = **Track.**

**Disposition:** *Needs work* → workstream C corpus ADR (R4 replaces the self-referential task; `corpus-discrimination-2026-07-07.md:264-286`).

**North-star relationship:** "Evidence, not vibes" — the guard is the tenet working; the self-referential task is the residual hole.

**Who judged it & how to check:** `grep -c answer_doc_contamination docs/evals/gate-run-2026-07-06-fixbatch/verdict.md` and read the 7 CONTAMINATION lines in that verdict.

**Cross-reference:** persona #6 "what good looks like," `research/02-pm-personalities.md:256` — "One clearly-owned … north star with … an explicit 'how this could be gamed' analysis." *Why it matters:* the TRUST ledger *is* that analysis; this finding measures how well it holds.

## What is actually fine (nulls — checked hard, found sound)

The metric *machinery* is, with real rigor, better than the flagship narrative around it. Each of these I tried to break and could not:

- **SPT is deliberately kept OUT of the gates.** `docs/adr/0016.6:99` — "SPT is deliberately not a gate; G1–G5 remain the verdict." The single most obvious Goodhart for a compression-heavy product — gating on the gameable SPT ratio — was **explicitly refused**. That is exactly right, and rare. (The finding in F4 is only that the *narrative* should say this out loud where it names the north star.)
- **G3 names its own gameability and requires joint G1/G2.** "a tiny useless report maximizes compression" (`fixbatch/verdict.md`, `confirmatory/verdict.md:33`) — the team knows the compression gate is meaningless alone and structurally couples it to correctness/evidence. Sophisticated metric design.
- **The ceiling problem is self-diagnosed, honestly, in writing.** `corpus-discrimination-2026-07-07.md` recomputes per-task discrimination from committed artifacts, ranks the tasks worst-first, states plainly "rests on **two** tasks," and **proposes a pre-registered fix path (R1–R4) with a closed-book canary before commit.** This is the North-Star Zealot's "what good looks like" — an explicit "how this could be gamed" analysis — done unprompted.
- **The honest negative is kept and cited.** `gate-run-2026-07/` (THESIS NOT SUPPORTED) is preserved and linked from M4 as the driver of the ADR-0016.7 fixes. M8/anticipation is honestly marked **FAILED** (`NORTH_STAR.md:283`), not buried. The doc does not cite a failed gate as evidence it works.
- **TRUST.md is a genuine, load-bearing trust ledger.** Every claim row names who verified it, from which artifact, when; every hole (H1–H8) is named, owned, and status-tracked; a memorization confound was *measured* (closed-book 0.067 vs open-book 0.926, H2) rather than assumed away. This is the discipline the metric-trust scope asks for, present and working.
- **The contamination guard works in production.** It fired, forced PRELIMINARY, and blocked a premature verdict until the sample was restored to contract (F6). Self-honesty machinery that actually fires is worth more than a clean-looking table.
- **Workstream traceability is real, not roadmap-as-backlog.** A/B/C sub-milestones map cleanly to M-numbers; the strategy chain (Vision → Path P1–P4 → Milestones → Workstreams → North-Star Filter) is operationalized with actual gate-keeping teeth — M4's G1/G3 "pauses P3/P4 spending" is a genuine Perri Target Condition, not decoration.

Net: the eval artifacts are more honest than the `NORTH_STAR.md` banner. The fix for F1–F5 is largely to make the flagship narrative say what the ledgers already say.

## Least-confident call

**F4** (named north star SPT is non-load-bearing while G1 actually gates). A fair reader can argue this is *correct* design, not a defect: the doc names the *aspiration* (SPT) and the gates *operationalize* it via correctness+compression, exactly as ADR-0016.6 intends. I kept it as a finding only because the *narrative* label "the optimization target is signals per token" would misdirect a new contributor toward the directly-moveable compression term (F1) — but the severity is deliberately low (Track), and I'd defer to Fable on whether it's a real misalignment or just a wording nit. Everything in F1–F3 I hold with high confidence; F5's "better exploration answers" contradiction is high-confidence, its "strategy theater" framing is medium.
