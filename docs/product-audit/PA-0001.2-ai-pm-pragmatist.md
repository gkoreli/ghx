## Abstract

I audited ghx as the **AI PM Pragmatist** (persona #15): my reflex is to
find capability claims with no calibrated eval behind them, "AI-powered" sold
as a feature, thin model-wrappers, and the assumption that today's model
ceiling is permanent. My signature question — *"what traced, calibrated eval
backs this capability claim, and who scored it, from what evidence?"* — is
unusually well-served here. ghx is the rare AI product where the eval
discipline is a first-class engineered workstream (C), verdicts are
pre-registered, a trust ledger names its own holes, the honest negative is
committed and linked from the README, and the quality judge is *deliberately
withheld from citation until it calibrates*. On the pragmatist's core test —
"do you cite evals you can't audit?" — ghx passes emphatically. Most of what I
attacked, I found sound (see the nulls section, which carries equal weight).

The findings that survive scrutiny are therefore not "they're faking it." They
are a narrower, sharper class: **the north-star's load-bearing capability claim
is phrased as a quality claim, while the eval that actually backs it today
measures compact fact-recall — and the quality layer that would close the gap
(the LLM judge, the combined-objective host-task eval) is decided but not yet
citable.** The gates prove ghx returns the *right facts* at ~16.6× compression
without catastrophic recall loss. They do **not** yet prove the sidecar
produces *better reconnaissance* or makes the main agent *better at
engineering* — the two things the north star's "both, not one" rebuttal
promises. The team knows this; the risk is that the prose (and anyone quoting
M4) rounds "measured fact-recall parity" up to "measured quality parity." Every
finding below ties to the Visibility/Truthfulness tenet: *who scored this, from
what evidence, and how do I check it myself.*

Severity is Likelihood × Impact on a 3×3. Disposition routes needs-work
findings to a workstream (A/B/C) or an ADR, never a bare owner+date.

## Ranked findings

### F1 — "The sidecar helps" (quality half) is asserted; only the compact-fact-recall half is measured

**Persona · Scope · Surface.** AI PM Pragmatist · North-Star capability-claims + Evals-as-user-research · `docs/NORTH_STAR.md`, `docs/evals/gate-run-2026-07-06-fixbatch/`, ADR-0023.1, ADR-0032.1.

**The claim (quoted).** NORTH_STAR frames the central rebuttal as a *combined*
capability claim: *"the main agent gets measurably better at its own objective
(engineering …) **and** receives better exploration answers than it would have
produced itself. Both, not one."* (`docs/NORTH_STAR.md:48-52`), and *"The claim
to measure is combined: engineering SPT and exploration SPT both rise, just by
using ghx"* (`:54-55`). M4 is recorded as **"THESIS SUPPORTED … sidecar
correctness 0.908 vs ghx 0.931 at 25× compression, 24× signal/token"**
(`:279`).

**The flaw (named).** Neither the "better exploration answers" nor the "better
at its own [engineering] objective" half is measured by the run that carries
the THESIS SUPPORTED verdict. What the gates measure is stated by the judge ADR
itself: *"The deterministic gates measure pre-registered fact recall; they
decide verdicts and always will"* (`docs/adr/0023.1-judge-scorer-decision.md:22-23`),
and TRUST hole H1 is blunt: *"Checks measure fact recall, not quality.
Substring gates can score a lucky mention"* (`docs/evals/TRUST.md:27`). The two
instruments that would substantiate the *quality* half are decided-but-not-run:
(a) the LLM judge — *"was this good reconnaissance"* (`ADR-0023.1:24`) — is
uncalibrated (F3); (b) the combined-objective host-task eval that would prove
"both, not one" is **decided but never executed**: C8 is *"Decided
(ADR-0032.1) … run is Goga-triggered"* (`docs/NORTH_STAR.md:331`). So the
engineering-SPT-rises claim has **zero** episodes behind it today. "The sidecar
helps [engineering]" is currently an argued rebuttal (NORTH_STAR "Why"
section), not a measured result — precisely the "AI-powered as a capability
claim with no eval" pattern this persona exists to flag, appearing here in the
steering doc rather than in marketing.

**Severity.** Likelihood Medium × Impact High → **Escalate.** Impact is high
because this *is* the thesis. Likelihood is Medium, not High, because the
measured half (compact recall parity at 16.6×) is real, non-trivial evidence
and the overclaim is one of framing/rounding, not fabrication.

**Disposition.** Needs-work → **workstream C** (run C8; unlock the judge, F3).
Not "invalidates": the gates legitimately support a *narrower* verdict. The
product fix is a wording discipline — state M4 as "compact fact-recall parity
at 16.6× compression," and reserve "the sidecar helps" for after C8 + judge κ.

**North-star relationship.** Directly bears on the north-star filter's own
evidence tenet (*"token efficiency and effectiveness are measured, never
assumed,"* `:180-181`) and the Visibility/Truthfulness duty. Passes the "make
reconnaissance more auditable" leg; the "more proficient" leg is asserted.

**Who judged it & how to check.** Re-derivable without me: read the frame line
in ADR-0023.1, H1 in TRUST.md, and C8's status line in NORTH_STAR; confirm no
`docs/evals/` directory contains a combined-objective / host-task run
(`ls docs/evals/` — none exists as of 2026-07-07). Cross-family check advisable
before this is cited in a milestone decision, per the audit's own directive 5.

**Cross-reference.** Marty Cagan, "AI Product Management 2 Years In," SVPG —
[svpg.com/ai-product-management-2-years-in/](https://www.svpg.com/ai-product-management-2-years-in/):
his running split between AI-as-enabling-technology and AI-as-changed-workflow,
and the warning that AI *amplifies* product judgment rather than substituting
for it — the exact distinction between "we argue the sidecar helps" and "we
measured that it does."

### F2 — "Correctness ≈ ghx" rests on 2 of 6 tasks; on the one task that can see a regression, the sidecar loses

**Persona · Scope · Surface.** AI PM Pragmatist · Evals-as-user-research (is the suite trustworthy user research?) · `docs/evals/corpus-discrimination-2026-07-07.md`, `gate-run-2026-07-06-fixbatch/verdict.md`, `docs/evals/TRUST.md` H7.

**The claim (quoted).** The citable run reports **"G1 sidecar/ghx correctness
ratio 0.983 (floor 0.90)"** with sidecar 0.930 vs ghx 0.946
(`gate-run-2026-07-06-fixbatch/README.md:19`, `verdict.md:15`) — read as
near-parity in correctness.

**The flaw (named).** The team's own discrimination audit shows that aggregate
is thin: *"The corpus's discrimination currently rests on two tasks
(express-router-location, openai-node-streaming). The other four are
ceiling-capped (gin, hono, flask) or contamination-confounded (ghx-mapengine);
flask-routing additionally fails the H2 memorization bar"*
(`corpus-discrimination-2026-07-07.md:290-293`). Four of six tasks pin all
three profiles at 1.000 / spread 0.000 — *"a sidecar correctness change cannot
show"* there (`:116-118`). Worse for the capability claim: on
`openai-node-streaming`, the corpus's **only real regression sentinel**, the
sidecar *underperforms* — sidecar 0.800 < plain 0.950 < ghx 1.000, sidecar−ghx
**−0.200** (`:76`, `:157-162`). So on the single task instrumented to reveal a
sidecar quality delta, compression costs correctness, and the 0.983 aggregate
*absorbs* it (TRUST H6 notes the `at_least(n)` reducer "exposes the
openai-node-streaming cell the run mean absorbs," `TRUST.md:34`). A "near-parity
correctness" number whose discriminating support is one task where the sidecar
loses is a weaker capability claim than the aggregate suggests.

**Severity.** Likelihood Medium × Impact High → **Escalate.** A downstream
reader treating 0.983 as "the sidecar is as correct as ghx" is materially
overreading; the impact is on the load-bearing correctness gate itself.

**Disposition.** Needs-work → **workstream C** — adopt the corpus-discrimination
doc's R1–R4 harder tasks as a **pre-registered eval-corpus ADR** (moves 2/6 →
projected 6/6 discriminating, `:294-297`), each gated by a closed-book canary
before commit. Until then, cite the per-task openai regression alongside the
aggregate, never the aggregate alone.

**North-star relationship.** The eval is the north star's answer to "why does
ghx deserve to exist" (`:106-108`); a corpus that can't discriminate on 4/6
tasks under-serves that job. Improving it is squarely "make the evidence more
auditable / trustworthy."

**Who judged it & how to check.** The doc is fully recompute-scripted
(`corpus-discrimination-2026-07-07.md:41-64`, zero-token jq over committed
JSON); I re-read the per-task table rather than trusting the summary. The
openai −0.200 reproduces from `.rewards.correctness` in the fixbatch episode
files.

**Cross-reference.** Kohavi/Tang/Xu, *Trustworthy Online Controlled
Experiments*, companion encyclopedia entry —
[exp-platform.com/Documents/2023-03-11EncyclopeiaMLDSABTestingFinal.pdf](https://exp-platform.com/Documents/2023-03-11EncyclopeiaMLDSABTestingFinal.pdf):
"getting numbers is easy; getting numbers you can trust is hard" — a
ceiling-capped instrument returns numbers you cannot trust to move when the
product changes.

### F3 — No quality score is citable: the judge is uncalibrated, and gates disagree with two model families 19% of the time (one-directionally)

**Persona · Scope · Surface.** AI PM Pragmatist · Trust & verifiability + Evals-as-user-research · `docs/adr/0023.1-judge-scorer-decision.md`, `docs/evals/judge-sweep-2026-07-06/SUMMARY.md` + `cross-family/SUMMARY.md`, `docs/evals/TRUST.md` H1.

**The claim (quoted).** The eval stack advertises a quality layer: a *"calibrated
judge scorer … to compare plain vs ghx vs ghx-sidecar reasoning, tool calls,
and outputs"* (`docs/NORTH_STAR.md:344-347`).

**The flaw (named) — but note it is honestly disclosed.** No judge score is
citable, by the team's own binding rule: *"No judge score is citable before: a
hand-labeled gold set … agreement κ ≥ 0.6 … committed under docs/evals/"*
(`ADR-0023.1:83-85`), and every judge artifact stamps itself *"PRELIMINARY —
NEVER CITABLE, NOT A QUALITY CLAIM"* (`judge-sweep-2026-07-06/SUMMARY.md:5`).
The one signal the sweeps *do* surface is a caution for the gates: **19.3% of
episodes are "gates fail, judge good" and 0% are "gates pass, judge poor"**
(`SUMMARY.md:104-108`) — a strictly one-directional disagreement above the 15%
INVESTIGATE bound, **cross-family confirmed 27/27** by GPT-5.5 with no
self-preference confound (`cross-family/SUMMARY.md:61-62`, `:68`). Read
plainly: the deterministic gates *reject* real, good exploration ~1 episode in
5 (they under-credit quality), which means "correctness 0.930" is a *noisy,
conservative* proxy for reconnaissance quality, not a measurement of it. The
capability claim that would use the judge ("the sidecar reasons better") has no
citable number at all today.

**Severity.** Likelihood Medium × Impact Medium → **Scoped fix / Track.** Impact
is bounded *because* the team refuses to cite the judge — the harm is only
realized if someone quotes a PRELIMINARY judge mean or reads the gate number as
a quality number.

**Disposition.** Needs-work → **workstream C4/C7** — the founder gold-set
labeling session is named as *"the sole remaining unlock for κ"*
(`TRUST.md:28`). This is on the roadmap; the finding is to keep the "NEVER
CITABLE" discipline intact and to not let F1/F2's quality framing borrow
credibility from an uncalibrated judge in the interim.

**North-star relationship.** "Evidence, not vibes" and "trust is a tracked
workstream, never assumed" (`:190-199`) — this finding *is* the tenet working
as designed; the pragmatist credit is that the hole is named, not hidden.

**Who judged it & how to check.** Read the D5 rule in ADR-0023.1, the NEVER
CITABLE banner in both sweep SUMMARYs, and the 19.3% cross-tab. The
one-directionality is re-derivable from the appendix per-episode table
(`SUMMARY.md:145-291`).

**Cross-reference.** Anish Acharya, "5 Principles for PMs … in the AI Era,"
a16z — [a16z.com/stay-relevant-in-ai/](https://a16z.com/stay-relevant-in-ai/):
"interview your models" — the judge sweep is exactly interviewing the judge
before trusting it; ghx does this and withholds the verdict, which is the
principle honored, not violated.

### F4 — Eager anticipation (M8) is described in vision prose as a working capability while its own gate FAILED near-zero

**Persona · Scope · Surface.** AI PM Pragmatist · North-Star capability-claims · `docs/NORTH_STAR.md` (Sidecar Product Capabilities §2), `docs/evals/anticipation-predictor-2026-07/README.md`, `internal/sidecar/report.go`.

**The claim (quoted).** The capabilities section describes anticipation in
confident, mechanism-level terms: *"the sidecar anticipates the follow-ups and
starts exploring before they are asked — capturing traces and reports so an
anticipated question answers in under a second instead of 30"*
(`docs/NORTH_STAR.md:135-139`), with a worked example of an instant CodeRabbit
competitor comparison (`:141-143`).

**The flaw (named).** The predictor that anticipation depends on does not work
yet: the D1 gate **FAILED** at *"micro recall 0.071"* (committed evals) and
*"0.000"* (dogfood) against a 0.30 threshold
(`anticipation-predictor-2026-07/README.md:13-14`, `:39-41`); the milestone row
concedes *"D1 gate FAILED — nextReads recall 0.071/0.000, field sparse in
practice; prefetch build correctly halted"* (`docs/NORTH_STAR.md:283`). The
`nextReads` field ships in the report schema (`internal/sidecar/report.go:56`),
so the plumbing exists — but it is non-predictive, so the capability described
in §2 is aspirational, and a reader who stops at the capabilities section
(three screens above the milestone table) would believe it works. This is the
"capability claim with no passing eval" pattern; the audit skill flags exactly
this case (*"Don't cite a milestone whose gate failed as evidence it works —
anticipation/M8,"* SKILL.md).

**Severity.** Likelihood Low × Impact Medium → **Track.** Low likelihood of
harm because the milestone table is honest, the build was correctly halted, and
this is an internal steering doc, not external marketing. It would jump to
Escalate the moment anticipation appears as "works today" in any customer-facing
surface.

**Disposition.** Needs-work → minor doc fix (mark §2 anticipation as
frontier/unproven inline, matching the honesty of the milestone row) — routes
to **workstream B9 / a NORTH_STAR edit**, not an ADR. The *process* around it
(gate failed → build halted → next step pre-registered) is sound.

**North-star relationship.** Anticipation is a P3 capability that must pass the
same north-star filter; presenting it as working before it does risks steering
build effort against an unvalidated need (product-sequencing YAGNI).

**Who judged it & how to check.** Read §2 prose against the M8 milestone row and
the predictor README's gate verdict — the contradiction is on the page.

**Cross-reference.** Melissa Perri, "The Build Trap" —
[melissaperri.com/blog/2014/08/05/the-build-trap](https://melissaperri.com/blog/2014/08/05/the-build-trap):
describing a capability as delivered before its outcome is validated is the
build-trap failure mode in miniature; the halted prefetch build is the
anti-pattern *avoided*, the prose is where it leaks back in.

### F5 — Headline efficiency numbers are proxy-derived and "correctness" is unlabeled as fact-recall

**Persona · Scope · Surface.** AI PM Pragmatist · Trust & verifiability · `docs/NORTH_STAR.md:279`, `docs/evals/TRUST.md` H5, `gate-run-2026-07-06-fixbatch/README.md`.

**The claim (quoted).** M4 quotes **"24× signal/token"** and correctness figures
inline as headline results (`docs/NORTH_STAR.md:279`).

**The flaw (named).** "Signal/token" is a `chars/4` proxy, not provider-reported
tokens: H5 — *"Token accounting is a chars/4 proxy, not provider-reported
tokens … SPT absolute values approximate (ratios robust)"* (`TRUST.md:33`) —
now promoted to a dual-reported real-token variant *"ALONGSIDE chars/4 — both
always shown, neither primary yet"* (`:33`). And "correctness," quoted beside
compression, is fact-recall correctness (F1/H1), not quality correctness, but
the milestone line carries no qualifier. Neither is wrong; both are *rounder
than the underlying measurement*, and a proxy number in the steering doc is the
kind of thing that migrates into a slide unqualified.

**Severity.** Likelihood Low × Impact Low → **Log.** The ratios are robust
(H5), the README already reads numbers as a "conservative floor" (see nulls),
and dual-reporting is landed. Minor.

**Disposition.** Needs-work → **workstream C** wording hygiene: when M4's
numbers are quoted, tag "signal/token" as chars/4-proxy and "correctness" as
fact-recall, matching the granularity TRUST.md already applies.

**North-star relationship.** "Every score recomputable … measurement stack
frozen" — labeling a proxy as a proxy is the same discipline one level up.

**Cross-reference.** Same Acharya a16z piece (above) — the discipline of not
overstating what a metric measures is the AI-era PM's core hygiene.

## What I checked and found SOUND (nulls — equal rigor)

The eval discipline here is genuinely stronger than almost any AI product I
would audit. I attacked each of the following expecting a crack and did not
find one:

1. **The judge is withheld from citation until calibrated — the pragmatist's
   ideal.** Every judge artifact self-stamps "NEVER CITABLE, NOT A QUALITY
   CLAIM" (`judge-sweep-2026-07-06/SUMMARY.md:5`); the κ ≥ 0.6 gate is *binding*
   and pre-committed (`ADR-0023.1:83-85`). A team that had an uncalibrated judge
   producing 4.8/5 means (`SUMMARY.md:81-84`) and *refused* to quote them is
   doing the opposite of "AI-powered with no eval."

2. **The honest negative is committed and linked from the public README.**
   `gate-run-2026-07/verdict.md` records "THESIS NOT SUPPORTED … sidecar 0.580"
   and the README links it explicitly (`README.md:134-136`). Negative results
   committed = the Visibility tenet honored where it costs something.

3. **Self-preference confound named and structurally addressed.** The same-family
   sweep flags its own confound (`SUMMARY.md:5-16`) and the cross-family GPT-5.5
   re-score removes it (`cross-family/SUMMARY.md:12-13`, 27/27 confirmed). The
   audit skill's own worry — a Claude judge favoring Claude output
   (arXiv:2404.13076) — is anticipated by the product's own eval design.

4. **Memorization confound measured, not assumed (H2).** Closed-book probe:
   corpus mean **0.067** vs 0.926 open-book, zero tool activity verified over
   committed artifacts (`memorization-audit/README.md:104`, `:34-38`). The one
   residual (flask 0.40) is named and first-in-line for replacement, not buried.
   This is "interview your model for its failure modes" done properly.

5. **Contamination guard fires and self-labels.** 7 episodes auto-excluded on the
   citable run, dropping the plain cell below minimum, forcing interim
   PRELIMINARY / THESIS NOT SUPPORTED until top-ups
   (`gate-run-2026-07-06-fixbatch/README.md:44-54`). The framework enforces
   honesty *against its own preferred outcome*.

6. **Independent cross-family recompute of all 90 episode scores, exact**
   (`README.md:124-128`; `audit-2026-07-05-independent/REPORT.md`) — deterministic
   scores are recomputable by a different model family, the strongest form of
   "how do I check it myself."

7. **The corpus's own weakness (2/6 discriminating) was found by the team, not by
   me** (`corpus-discrimination-2026-07-07.md`) — a self-audit that pre-empts F2.
   A red-team that only re-states the target's own committed self-critique is
   corroborating rigor, not finding a hole; I credit the discipline and route the
   product implication.

8. **README reads numbers as a "conservative floor" and links verdicts, not
   headlines** (`README.md:130-134`) — the external surface is *more* disciplined
   than the internal NORTH_STAR prose, which is the right direction.

9. **The report schema makes confidently-wrong output partly self-distinguishing.**
   `Report` separates `Verified` / `Inferred` / `Unverified` claims, `CommandsRun`,
   `Evidence`, `Uncertainty` (`internal/sidecar/report.go:38-57`), and the recon
   skill tells the consumer "trust verified … treat inferred/unverified as leads"
   (`skills/ghx-recon/SKILL.md:41-43`). A downstream agent can weight claims by
   the producer's own confidence tier — the evidence-not-vibes tenet made
   structural. (Whether the *tiering itself* is honest is what the judge's
   uncertainty-honesty dimension is meant to measure — i.e. gated on F3.)

**Blind-spot disclosure (persona honesty).** My lens over-indexes on "is it
measured?" and can undervalue that an *argued* rebuttal (NORTH_STAR "Why") is
legitimate pre-measurement reasoning, not a lie; F1 is a framing/rounding
finding, not a fabrication finding, and I have marked it so. I also did not
audit the token-economics SPT math, the runtime resilience (ADR-0027), or the
CLI/AX surface — those belong to other personas and the code audits.
